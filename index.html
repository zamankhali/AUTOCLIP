Upload. Speak. Done.

Free, runs entirely in this browser tab. Auto-frame uses real face detection. Captions are transcribed live from your video's audio through your microphone — the only transcription method that costs nothing and needs no server.

How it works
1

Upload your video below.

2

Tap Start. Your video will play with sound — turn your volume up and be somewhere quiet. Your phone's mic listens and transcribes as it plays.

3

The app auto-frames to vertical and burns in word-by-word captions live, cycling red / green / white.

4

When the video ends, download the finished clip.

This is a genuine limitation of doing this for free with no server: the mic has to "hear" your video play out loud, so accuracy depends on your speaker volume and room noise. If you want higher accuracy later, that requires a paid transcription API — I can add that as an option once you're ready to spend a little on it.
